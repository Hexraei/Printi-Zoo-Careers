UI/UX Designer

Making one complicated local commerce workflow feel obvious to three very different kinds of users.

What you'll do

  • Design simple, intuitive interfaces for shop owners, customers, and delivery partners
  • Turn the ordering and delivery workflow into screens a first-time smartphone user can pick up without a manual
  • Build and maintain a lightweight design system that works across all three apps
  • Run quick, real-world tests with actual shop owners and delivery partners
  • Work directly with developers so your designs ship the way you meant them to

This is an early-stage startup role. We care a lot more about your curiosity and what you've actually built than how many years are on your resume.
