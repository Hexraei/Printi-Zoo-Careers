The mission

Delivery, built for the shop next door.

Most instant-delivery apps run out of a handful of warehouses stocked with their own catalog, so they only reach the neighbourhoods those warehouses were built for. Printi Zoo works differently. We partner directly with shops that are already open in every town and street across Tamil Nadu: departmental stores, local retailers, family-run businesses, and we give each of them a delivery channel of their own.

Shop owners get a new way to sell without changing how their store runs. Delivery partners get steady, commission-based work close to home. And customers get access to the shops that were always around the corner but never had a way to deliver, including the areas that warehouse-only delivery services don't reach at all.
